Questions?

The answers to what people ask us most.

How do I pay?

We use Yoco for all payments. You'll get a secure payment link when you sign up. Card, Google Pay, and Instant EFT all work.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts and no cancellation fees. You can cancel from your account page any time — you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

What happens when I upgrade?

Your new plan starts immediately after approval. Upgrading charges the new plan's full monthly price — the remaining time on your old plan isn't credited. See full details in our terms.

Can I share my account?

Your plan includes a fixed number of simultaneous devices. Standard lets 2 people watch at once, Premium 3, Elite 4. You can use the same account on as many devices as you want — only the number of devices watching at the same time is limited.

What about profiles?

Each plan includes a number of profiles — personal watch spaces under one login. Free & Starter have 1, Standard has 2, Premium has 3, Elite has 4. Perfect for families sharing one account.

What devices are supported?

Any modern phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop browser. Just sign in and start watching.

Is there a free trial?

The Free plan is our trial. Watch with ads, get a feel for the platform, then upgrade whenever you're ready — no credit card needed to start.

What if I'm not happy?

If something's broken and we can't fix it, we'll refund you within 7 days. Contact support@starlanestream.com.
