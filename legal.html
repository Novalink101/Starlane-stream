Terms of Service

These Terms govern your use of Starlane Stream and your subscription.

1. Who we are

Starlane Stream ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Starlane Stream Pty Ltd, registered in the Republic of South Africa.

Starlane Stream is a subscription streaming platform offering live TV, movies, sports, and on-demand content. You can browse our catalogue for free and upgrade to a paid plan at any time.

2. Your account

You may create an account using Google sign-in or an email and password. You agree to:

  • Provide accurate, current, and complete information when registering.
  • Keep your login credentials secure and confidential.
  • Accept responsibility for all activity that occurs under your account.
  • Notify us immediately at support@starlanestream.com if you suspect unauthorised access.

You must be at least 18 years old to subscribe to a paid plan. Free viewers under 18 may use the platform with parental supervision.

3. Subscription plans and billing

Starlane Stream offers the following plans:

  • Free — R0/month, ad-supported, 480p, 1 device.
  • Starter — R90/month, no ads, 720p, 1 device.
  • Standard — R180/month, no ads, 1080p, 2 devices.
  • Premium — R300/month, no ads, 4K, 3 devices.
  • Elite — R490/month, no ads, 4K, 4 devices, family profiles.

Billing

  • Paid subscriptions are billed monthly in advance unless you choose annual billing.
  • Payment is processed via Yoco — a PCI-DSS-compliant South African payment provider.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel beforehand.
  • We do not store your full card details. Payment information is handled entirely by Yoco.

Cancellation

  • You may cancel at any time from your account page.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep access until then.
  • We do not offer partial refunds for unused time.

4. Free tier and ads

The Free plan is ad-supported. Ads may play before, during, or after streams. You cannot skip ads on the Free plan.

Upgrading to any paid plan removes all ads immediately.

5. Device limits

Each plan includes a maximum number of simultaneous devices that can stream at the same time:

  • Free / Starter: 1 device
  • Standard: 2 devices
  • Premium: 3 devices
  • Elite: 4 devices

You may sign in on as many devices as you like — but only the specified number can stream at once. If you exceed the limit, the newest device will be blocked until another stops.

Account sharing with people outside your household is not permitted. We may suspend accounts that repeatedly exceed reasonable use.

6. Content and intellectual property

All content on Starlane Stream — including videos, graphics, logos, and interface — is owned by Starlane Stream or its licensors and is protected by copyright law.

You may not:

  • Download, record, or redistribute any content without written permission.
  • Bypass any geographic, subscription, or access control.
  • Use automation (bots, scrapers) against the platform.
  • Attempt to reverse-engineer, decompile, or hack the service.
Streams played on paid plans are watermarked with your account email. This deters piracy and lets us trace leaked content back to the source.

7. Refunds

If the service is broken and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, we will refund you within 7 days of your payment.

Refunds are not issued for:

  • Change of mind after subscribing.
  • Failure to use the service during your subscription period.
  • Device compatibility issues listed on our supported devices page.
  • Content you expected but which is not available on the platform.

Contact support@starlanestream.com to request a refund.

8. Service availability

We aim for 99% uptime but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. Scheduled maintenance, third-party outages, and content licensing changes may interrupt streams.

9. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Starlane Stream is not liable for:

  • Indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
  • Loss of data, revenue, or business opportunity.
  • Interruption of service caused by events outside our control.

Where liability cannot be excluded, our total liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the 3 months preceding the claim.

10. Termination

We may suspend or terminate your account if you breach these Terms, if we are legally required to, or if we reasonably believe your use creates risk. You may delete your account at any time.

Privacy Policy

How Starlane Stream collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. Who we are

Starlane Stream Pty Ltd is the Responsible Party for personal information collected through the Platform, as defined by the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

2. What we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Account info — name, email address, password (encrypted), profile photo (if Google sign-in).
  • Subscription info — plan tier, start date, expiry, payment reference.
  • Payment info — payment reference, amount, timestamp. We never store full card numbers.
  • Device data — device ID, browser type, IP address (hashed) for concurrent-stream tracking.
  • Watch history — which channels and streams you've watched, timestamps.
  • Communications — support emails and messages you send us.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process subscription payments and manage billing.
  • Enforce device limits and prevent account sharing.
  • Send transaction emails (payment confirmations, plan changes).
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and piracy.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Improve the service using aggregated, de-identified analytics.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Lawful basis (POPIA)

  • Consent — where you have given us clear permission.
  • Contract — to perform the subscription agreement you entered into.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with tax, consumer protection, and other laws.
  • Legitimate interest — to prevent fraud and secure the platform.

5. Who we share information with

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication and database hosting. Located in the United States.
  • Yoco — payment processing. Located in South Africa.
  • EmailJS — transactional email delivery. Located in the United States.
  • Render — static site hosting. Located in the United States.
  • Law enforcement — where required by law or court order.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data — while your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion.
  • Subscription records — 5 years (tax/financial law).
  • Watch history — 24 months.
  • Support messages — 24 months.
  • Technical logs — 90 days.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) on all connections.
  • Password hashing and secure authentication via Firebase.
  • Firestore security rules restricting access by role.
  • Payment data handled entirely by Yoco (PCI-DSS compliant).

If we become aware of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by Section 22 of POPIA.

8. Cross-border transfers

Because we use Firebase and EmailJS (both located in the US), some of your information is transferred outside South Africa. Section 72 of POPIA allows such transfers where the recipient provides adequate protection. We rely on Google's and EmailJS's standard contractual clauses.

9. Cookies and local storage

  • Essential cookies — required to keep you signed in (Firebase Authentication).
  • Local storage — device ID for concurrent-stream tracking. Stays on your device.

We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Object to processing on reasonable grounds.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

See our POPIA Notice for how to exercise these rights.

11. Children

You must be at least 18 to create a paid subscription. Free viewers under 18 may use the platform with parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
