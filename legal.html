Terms of Service

Effective: 20 October 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

Starlane Stream ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Starlane Stream Pty Ltd, registered in the Republic of South Africa.

Starlane Stream is a streaming platform offering live events, sports, and on-demand content. You can browse our catalogue for free and pay to unlock specific events.

2. Your Account

You may create an account using Google sign-in or an email and password. You agree to:

  • Provide accurate, current, and complete information when registering.
  • Keep your login credentials secure and confidential.
  • Accept responsibility for all activity that occurs under your account.

3. Payments and Access

Some events on Starlane Stream require a ticket or Pay-Per-View (PPV) pass. Payments are processed securely by Yoco.

  • Novalink Tickets: If you purchase a ticket via Novalink, clicking the "Watch on Starlane" link will grant you access.
  • PPV Passes: You may purchase direct access to a stream. This is a one-time digital purchase.
  • Device Limits: Access is locked to the first device used to verify a ticket. Sharing links will result in access being blocked.

4. Refunds

Because digital streams are consumed instantly upon access, we do not offer refunds for PPV purchases if the stream is working. If the stream is broken and we cannot fix it, we will refund you within 7 days.

Security Notice: Streams are watermarked with your account email. This deters piracy and allows us to trace leaked content back to the source.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 October 2026 · Version 1.0

1. What we collect

  • Account info — name, email address, password (encrypted).
  • Payment info — payment reference, amount, timestamp. We never store full card numbers.
  • Device data — device ID, browser type, IP address (hashed) for concurrent-stream tracking.
  • Watch history — which events and streams you've watched.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process payments and manage access.
  • Enforce device limits and prevent account sharing.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and piracy.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Who we share information with

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication and database hosting. Located in the US.
  • Yoco — payment processing. Located in South Africa.
  • Render — web hosting. Located in the US.

POPIA Notice

Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013

1. Your Rights under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Object to processing on reasonable grounds.

2. How to exercise these rights

Email privacy@starlanestream.com with your full name, the email address on your account, and what right you want to exercise. We will respond within 30 days.

3. The Information Regulator

If you are not satisfied with how we handled your personal information, you have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa:

  • Email: complaints.IR@justice.gov.za
  • Website: inforegulator.org.za
